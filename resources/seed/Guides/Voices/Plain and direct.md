# Plain and direct

A sample voice guide. Edit it to describe how you write; the AI reads it when it drafts or rewrites.

## Who we write for

Busy, capable readers who want the point first. They know their field; they do not know our project.

## How it sounds

- Lead with the conclusion, then the reasons.
- One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 20 words.
- Concrete over abstract: name the thing, the number, the date.
- Active voice. Say who does what.
- Friendly through clarity, not through adjectives or exclamation marks.

## Avoid

- Hype words: innovative, cutting-edge, world-class, seamless.
- Hedging a position we hold: “you might consider”, “perhaps”.
- Explaining the obvious or repeating the heading.
